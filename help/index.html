What are films and moments?

A film is what you make in oblivious: an ordered run of moments, with an optional soundtrack over it.

A moment is one photo, or one short piece of video. Every moment plays for the same length, so a film reads as a rhythm rather than a timeline.

  • A film can hold up to 60 moments.
  • moment length is 1 or 2 seconds, chosen per film under moment duration.
  • Order carries meaning. Reordering the moments changes how the film reads.

How do I add moments?

Open the film and tap ＋. Photos and videos come in the order you picked them, and that becomes the order of the moments.

If only some of your library shows up, oblivious has access to a selected set of photos. Tap Select more photos to widen that set, or Change settings to allow all photos.

  • A video shorter than the moment duration becomes a single still frame.
  • A moment refers back to the item in your photo library. Delete the original there and the moment has nothing left to show.

What can I do when editing a moment?

Tap a moment to open it for editing. Changes stay staged until you tap Done, and closing instead lets you throw them away.

  • Pinch and drag to choose how the picture sits in the frame. Fill fills the frame in one tap, and Reset returns to unfilled.
  • For a video, the filmstrip picks which part plays.
  • Burn in a short line of text, and stamp the capture date and time.
  • Choose whether a video keeps its own sound.
  • Previous moment ghost shows the moment before, faintly, for lining the two up.
  • Duplicate before and Duplicate after in the ⋯ menu add a copy of this moment before or after it, carrying over everything you've set. For a video, the copy takes the stretch of footage just before or just after. (from 2.0.1)
  • Remove moment, in the same ⋯ menu, takes it out of the film. The photo or video in your library isn't touched.

What can I change about the film itself?

Open the film and tap ⋯ at the top. What's in there acts on the whole film, not on a single moment.

  • 1 sec per moment / 2 sec per moment sets how long every moment plays.
  • Sort by reorders the whole film at once, by Capture date or Date added. Dragging rows by hand still works afterwards.
  • Edit name renames the film.
  • Change aspect ratio picks the shape of the frame — 16:9, 3:2, 4:3 or 1:1, portrait or landscape. Each moment keeps whether it was filled or unfilled, but position and zoom go back to the start. (keeping filled / unfilled is from 2.0.1)
  • Delete film can't be undone. The photos and videos in your library aren't touched — but if other films are using a soundtrack created from this one, it goes from them too.

How do I save or share a film?

When the film plays the way you want, tap Save Video to open the save screen. Saving and sharing both happen here.

The preview is exactly what will come out — the film at its orientation, with the soundtrack mixed in. You can also put a color finish (a “look”) on the video, chosen from 13 built-in LUTs, with adjustable strength. Tap the picture to briefly see it without the finish.

  • Save writes the video to your photo library. The share button at the top hands the same video to another app.
  • A color finish goes onto the video you export, not onto the film itself. The screen always opens at Original.
  • Saving changes nothing about the film. You can keep editing and save again.

How does creating a soundtrack work?

In Choose soundtrack you can take one of the tracks that ship with oblivious, use audio from your device, or pick Generate soundtrack, where oblivious reads the film and writes a track for it.

  • Each film can generate its own track once.
  • You can create 3 soundtracks a month in total, counted across every film. Settings shows how many are left and when the count resets.
  • A track created in one film can be chosen from another film. Reusing one costs nothing.
  • Creating needs a network connection. It keeps running if you leave the app, and you can stop it while it runs.

What does iCloud sync cover?

iCloud sync stays off until you turn it on in Settings, under Sync across devices. With it on, your films travel between devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

  • Carried across: films — their moments, order, trims, name and orientation — and the soundtrack attached to each one.
  • Not carried across: the photos and videos themselves. A moment points at your photo library, so the other device needs iCloud Photos on to show the same picture.
  • A video you saved lives in your photo library like any other video, and travels the same way.

Why does the screen dim on bright scenes?

This isn't coming from oblivious. It's a setting on your iPhone called Dim Flashing Lights, which dims the screen when it detects large, sudden changes in brightness, to ease the strain on your eyes.

Because a film cuts between scenes, it tends to kick in the instant a dark scene turns bright, or on bright footage that shimmers, like moving water. It acts on the display rather than on the video, so the same thing happens in other apps, including Photos.

To turn it off: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Dim Flashing Lights.

It's a safeguard for people who are sensitive to flashing lights, so whether to turn it off is your call. The exact wording can vary by iOS version.

A moment won't load — what happened?

A moment points at a photo or video in your photo library. When oblivious can't reach that original, the row says Couldn't load, or Deleted from camera roll when the original can't be found in the library at all.

  • Put the original back and the moment comes back. If you deleted it, Photos keeps it under Recently Deleted for a while. If iCloud Photos simply hasn't brought it down yet, it returns on its own.
  • It may only be out of reach on this device. oblivious sees a selected set of photos, iCloud Photos is off, or a photo or video from another device hasn't synced yet — any of these can read as Deleted from camera roll. Open Photos and check the original is there.
  • Leaving it is fine. A moment that can't load is skipped — it doesn't play, and it isn't in the video you save. The film closes up around it.
  • It can't be edited while it's in this state, so tapping the row does nothing.
  • To remove it, swipe the row in the moment list and delete it.

Something isn't working — what can I check?

  • moments won't add: check the film hasn't reached 60 moments, and that oblivious can see the items you're picking.
  • A soundtrack won't generate: creating needs a network connection. If this month's 3 are used up, Settings shows when the count resets.
  • films aren't reaching another device: check sync across devices is on for both, signed in to the same Apple Account, and give the other device a moment to catch up.
