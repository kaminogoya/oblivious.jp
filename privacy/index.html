privacy

oblivious ("the app," "we," "us") is a film-making app that lets you weave moments from your photos and videos into a film. We designed oblivious so that your content never reaches us. This policy explains the limited information the app handles and why.

Your content never reaches us

Your photos, videos, the films you make, and any text you add to a moment are stored on your device, and we do not upload, collect, or store your media or your text on our servers. Saving a finished film to your photo library or sharing it with others happens only when you choose to, through your device's own save and share features — it goes to whomever you send it, never to us.

Photo library access

The app accesses your photo library only with your permission, and only to let you select moments and save finished films. Your selections are processed on your device.

Soundtrack generation

Only when you create a soundtrack does the app send an abstract description of that film to our server so a soundtrack can be composed for it.

  • What is sent: numbers and on/off values, such as brightness, motion, and warmth, plus a short list of mood words chosen from a fixed vocabulary. These are produced on your device using only the features needed to compose music. The app also sends anonymous identification data used to manage the monthly generation limit and prevent unauthorized use. This data is not linked to your name or email address.
  • What is not sent: the contents of your photos, videos, titles, or notes; identity information such as your name or email address; or raw data such as capture dates or location. You do not need to create an account or sign in with an email address or password.
  • What it is used for: the description is processed within the system that creates the soundtrack—our servers and the cloud and generative-music services we use—only as necessary to generate and deliver the soundtrack and operate the service safely. It is not used to train generative AI models or improve products. Your photos, videos, and text are never sent to these servers.

Logs

When the app generates a soundtrack or checks your remaining quota, it connects to our server. In doing so, our server and the cloud infrastructure we use keep records of the request—such as IP address, time, and error details—for a limited period. These are used only to prevent abuse, diagnose problems, and keep the service running; they contain none of the contents of your photos, videos, or text, and are never used for sale or advertising.

Support inquiries

If you contact us for support, we use your email address and the information you include in your inquiry only to respond to you.

No sale or advertising

We do not sell any information handled by the app—including the abstract description sent to generate a soundtrack—to third parties, or use it for advertising. The same applies to information we receive through support inquiries.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will update the effective date below.
