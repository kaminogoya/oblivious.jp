privacy

oblivious ("the app," "we," "us") is a film-making app that lets you weave moments from your photos and videos into a film. We designed oblivious so that your content never reaches us. This policy explains the limited information the app handles and why.

Your content never reaches us

Your photos, videos, the films you make, and any text you add to a moment are stored on your device, and we do not upload, collect, or store your media or your text on our servers. Saving a finished film to your photo library or sharing it with others happens only when you choose to, through your device's own save and share features — it goes to whomever you send it, never to us. The one thing you can deliberately send us is a message you type into the contact form, described below.

Photo library access

The app accesses your photo library only with your permission, and only to let you select moments and save finished films. Your selections are processed on your device.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your films are kept in your own iCloud so your devices match — film titles, the text you add to moments, and soundtrack audio. That storage sits in your own Apple Account, not on our server, so we cannot read it. We do not copy your photos or videos there; only references to items in your photo library are synced, and the media itself is handled by your own iCloud Photos if you use it. Turning the switch off stops that device from syncing.

Soundtrack generation

Only when you create a soundtrack does the app send an abstract description of that film to our server so a soundtrack can be composed for it.

  • What is sent: numbers and on/off values, such as brightness, motion, and warmth, plus a short list of mood words chosen from a fixed vocabulary. These are produced on your device using only the features needed to compose music. The app also sends anonymous identification data used to manage the monthly generation limit and prevent unauthorized use. This data is not linked to your name or email address.
  • What is not sent: the contents of your photos, videos, titles, or notes; identity information such as your name or email address; or raw data such as capture dates or location. You do not need to create an account or sign in with an email address or password.
  • What it is used for: the description is processed within the system that creates the soundtrack—our servers and the cloud and generative-music services we use—only as necessary to generate and deliver the soundtrack and operate the service safely. It is not used to train generative AI models or improve products. Your photos, videos, and text are never sent to these servers.

Logs

When the app generates a soundtrack, checks your remaining quota, or sends a message from the contact form, it connects to our server. In doing so, our server and the cloud infrastructure we use keep records of the request—such as IP address, time, and error details—for a limited period. A record of a contact form submission also includes the anonymous identification data and the app and iOS versions, but not the message you wrote or the address you entered. These are used only to prevent abuse, diagnose problems, and keep the service running; they contain none of the contents of your photos, videos, or text, and are never used for sale or advertising.

Support inquiries

The contact form in the app sends only the message you write. An email address is optional: you can send the form with that field empty, and then we have no way to identify you or to reply. We never send anything to an address you enter—if you asked for a reply, a person writes to you by hand.

  • What is sent: the message you write, and—only if you fill it in—an email address for a reply. The app also sends its own version, your iOS version, and the same anonymous identification data used for the soundtrack limit, so we can investigate problems. That data is not linked to your name, and it changes if you reinstall the app.
  • How it is kept: messages are stored on Google Cloud (Firestore, Tokyo region) as our processor, and are deleted automatically 90 days after they arrive. If you left the email field empty, no address is stored—not even a blank one.

No sale or advertising

We do not sell any information handled by the app—including the abstract description sent to generate a soundtrack—to third parties, or use it for advertising. The same applies to information we receive through support inquiries.
